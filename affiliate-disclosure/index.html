Affiliate disclosure

Last updated October 23, 2026

Bag Guy is free to download. To keep it that way, we may earn money from affiliate links, and in the future from optional memberships. Here's how that works.

What an affiliate link is

Some links in the Bag Guy app or on this website may be affiliate links. If you tap one and then make a purchase or sign up, Bag Guy may earn a commission from that company. It never costs you anything extra. The price you pay is the same whether or not you use our link.

How we keep it honest

  • We label affiliate links and sponsored placements clearly where they appear.
  • Our SNAP savings guide is written to be useful first. Guide articles don't depend on affiliate commissions.
  • We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share your SNAP status, receipts, or shopping history with affiliate partners.
  • SNAP benefits can only be used for eligible food. Any fees on another company's service, like delivery or service fees, are set by that company, can't be paid with SNAP, and are shown before you check out.

Today

As of the date above, Bag Guy may include links to grocery stores and shopping services. When an affiliate relationship applies, we'll say so next to the link.

Questions

Email support@bagguyapp.com.

This disclosure is provided in line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255).
