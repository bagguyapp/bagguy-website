About

Groceries are the budget line that can't wait.

Bag Guy is an independent app built to help SNAP and EBT shoppers, and anyone watching the grocery budget, find deals that actually fit their week. Make every grocery dollar go further.

What we believe

  • Respect first. Shopping with SNAP is normal. Bag Guy is designed to be clear, calm, and judgment-free.
  • Honest numbers. We don't make savings promises we can't back up. Example figures are labeled as examples.
  • Your benefits are yours. We never ask for your EBT card number, PIN, or benefits login, and we never share your SNAP status with advertisers.
  • Useful for free. The core app and our SNAP savings guide are free.

How Bag Guy makes money

Bag Guy may earn commissions from affiliate links, and plans to offer optional paid memberships in the future. These never change the price you pay at a store. Read our affiliate disclosure.

Independent, not official

Bag Guy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the USDA, SNAP, or any state agency, and it doesn't process EBT payments or manage benefits. Our guides cite USDA sources so you can check the facts yourself.

Get in touch

Questions, ideas, or a deal we got wrong? Email support@bagguyapp.com.
