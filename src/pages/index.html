What Bag Guy does

Less coupon hunting. More groceries covered.

Every feature below is labeled honestly: In the app means you can use it today. Next update means it is built and being tested for the next App Store release.

In the app

Scan your receipt

Snap a photo of your grocery receipt. Bag Guy reads it, looks for matching offers, and adds Bag Points once the scan is checked.

In the app

Find SNAP-friendly deals

Browse grocery and household offers, filter for SNAP-friendly items, and save the ones you want. Offers vary by area; always check eligibility at the register.

In the app

Scan grocery barcodes

Point your camera at a product barcode to check for matching deals. Our catalog is growing, so not every item will have a deal yet.

In the app

Earn Bag Points

Earn Bag Points for scanning receipts and saving offers. Points are counted and confirmed on our servers, so the balance you see is real. Bag Points have no cash value.

Built for SNAP & EBT shoppers

Made with SNAP families in mind. With respect.

"SNAP-friendly" in Bag Guy means an item is usually SNAP-eligible under federal rules. Some states now limit items like soda and candy, so when we're not sure, the app says so instead of guessing.

We don't touch your benefits. Bag Guy never asks for your EBT card number, PIN, or state portal login.
Your SNAP status stays private. We never sell it or share it with advertisers.
Not a government app. Bag Guy is independent and not affiliated with the USDA or any state agency.

FAQ

Good questions

Is Bag Guy a government or SNAP app?

No. Bag Guy is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the USDA, SNAP, or any state agency. To apply for SNAP or ask about your benefits, contact your state SNAP agency.

Can I pay with my EBT card in Bag Guy?

No. Bag Guy doesn't process payments or benefits. You pay at the store or on the store's own website the way you normally do.

Does "SNAP-friendly" mean an item will always be covered?

No. It means the item is usually SNAP-eligible under federal rules. Eligibility is decided at the register, and some states restrict items like soda and candy. See What SNAP covers.

Are Bag Points worth money?

Bag Points have no cash value and can't be exchanged for cash. If we add ways to redeem points, we'll explain them clearly in the app first.

How much will I save?

It depends on what you buy, where you shop, and which offers are available in your area. We won't promise a number we can't back up. Any figures shown on this website are examples.

Does Bag Guy have Android?

Bag Guy is on iPhone today. Android isn't available yet.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account for the steps and what happens to your data.
