How Bag Guy works

Bag Guy is a free iPhone app that helps you check grocery prices, spot SNAP-eligible items, and keep track of your Bag Points. Here's exactly what it does today, and what's next.

1. Create your account

Download Bag Guy from the App Store and sign up with your email. We ask only for what we need to run your account. We never ask for your EBT card number, PIN, or state benefits login, and you should never give those to any app.

2. Scan a barcode before you buy

Tap Scan and point your camera at a product barcode. When our catalog has the item, Bag Guy shows the product, a current shelf price, and whether it's marked SNAP-eligible. Our catalog is growing, so not every item is covered yet, and prices can differ at your store.

3. Shop the way you already do

Bag Guy doesn't process payments and doesn't handle SNAP or EBT benefits. Pay at the store, or on the store's own website, the way you normally do.

4. Keep track of your Bag Points

Your Bag Points balance appears on your profile. Points are confirmed on our servers, and more ways to earn arrive with deals and receipt reading. Bag Points are a thank-you for using Bag Guy. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged for cash. If we add ways to redeem points, we'll explain the rules clearly in the app first.

Coming in the next update

These features are built and being tested for the next App Store release:

  • SNAP-friendly deals: browse current grocery offers, see which items are usually SNAP-eligible under federal rules, and save the ones you want for your trip.
  • Weekly budget helper: enter your own weekly grocery budget and see priced SNAP-friendly deals that fit it.
  • Stores that accept SNAP EBT: find nearby stores using the USDA's public retailer data.
  • Synced shopping lists: your list stays with your account across devices.
  • In-app account deletion: delete your account and data from Settings.
About "SNAP-friendly." It's a helpful label, not a promise. Eligibility is decided at the register, and some states restrict items like soda and candy. When we're not sure, the app will show "Check at register." Learn more in What SNAP covers.

Still in development

  • Receipt scanning: take a photo of your receipt after you shop, and Bag Guy will read the items, check them against available offers, and add Bag Points once the scan is checked. Before a receipt photo is sent to an outside text-recognition service, the app will ask for your OK. It isn't available in the app yet.
